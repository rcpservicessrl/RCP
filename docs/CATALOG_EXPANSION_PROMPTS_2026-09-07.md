## Shared prompt for the thirteen new generations

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one polished 2.5D illustration for an RCP Services business service catalog card, square 1024x1024. Match our existing family of dimensional business objects: warm amber yellow #FCB53F, creamy ivory white, charcoal black edge details, tiny muted sage accents; generously rounded beveled objects, subtle realistic material texture and highlights, soft studio illumination, compact three-quarter isometric view. Subject occupies 75% of canvas, completely visible, generous clear margin, readily understood at 220px. Genuinely transparent background with alpha; no background panel, no floor plane, no opaque backdrop. No words, letters, numbers, logos or watermark. No realistic people or hands. Do not make an app icon tile.

## Proposed RCP offer

POS Esencial implementation from RD$9,900 once; optional RCP accompaniment
from RD$1,990/month. The monthly accompaniment is not the POS license price.
Scope: one location, one register, up to three users, product/customer
catalog, sales, inventory, cash closing and reports; initial configuration,
import of up to 300 products from an agreed template and one two-hour remote
training session. Support scope and service hours are agreed in writing.

Electronic invoicing activation requires the taxpayer's habilitation,
certificate and selected provider. Provider subscriptions, e-CF consumption,
certificate/renewals, hardware, extra locations, custom integrations,
on-site visits and applicable taxes are quoted separately. No unlimited e-CF,
certification guarantee, fixed activation date or automatic payment is offered.

Price positioning is an implementation/support package in Dominican pesos,
not an assertion that it includes every feature or fiscal fee of the compared
subscriptions. A written quote must show the complete recurring and setup cost.

## Regulatory source

status. No certification or live fiscal transaction was performed in this task.
